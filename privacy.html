Information we collect

Information reaches The Ivy Sanctum in several ways: directly from you, through website interactions, through service providers used to operate the site, and through transactions you choose to complete.

Information you provide

When you submit an Office Hours question, send correspondence, inquire about advisory work, purchase a Library edition, or otherwise communicate with us, information may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Grade, year, or academic stage
  • Target schools or programs
  • Questions and written correspondence
  • Application-related information you choose to provide
  • Order and transaction information

Please exercise judgment when sharing application materials or personal circumstances and provide information reasonably connected to the inquiry or service requested.

Information generated through website use

Website and analytics technologies may generate information concerning page visits, navigation, interactions, approximate geographic area, referring source, browser, operating system, device type, and similar technical information.

How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to Office Hours questions and correspondence
  • Provide and administer advisory engagements
  • Process and fulfill Library purchases
  • Send transactional and service-related email
  • Understand website use and improve navigation
  • Measure interest in publications, services, and content
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse or fraud
  • Maintain accounting and transaction records
  • Comply with legal, tax, regulatory, and contractual obligations

Depending on the jurisdiction and context, processing may rely on consent, contractual necessity, legitimate interests, or compliance with legal obligations.

Office Hours and correspondence

The Office Hours form collects the information entered into the form, including name, email address, grade or year, target schools when supplied, and the question submitted.

Office Hours submissions are processed through Formspree and routed to The Ivy Sanctum for review and response.

Email correspondence may be transmitted using our branded email infrastructure and service providers, including Brevo.

Library purchases and payments

Library purchases are processed through Stripe. Payment information entered into Stripe Checkout is handled by Stripe according to its own privacy and security practices.

The Ivy Sanctum may receive transaction information associated with a purchase, including the purchaser's email address, product purchased, amount, payment status, date, and related order information required for fulfillment, accounting, customer support, refunds, and dispute handling.

Full payment-card credentials are processed through Stripe's payment infrastructure.

Website analytics

The Ivy Sanctum uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors discover and use the website.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics may process information including page views, sessions, approximate geographic information, referring source, browser and device characteristics, and interactions measured through the website.

We also use event measurement to understand activity such as entering the Library, opening a volume, expressing interest in forthcoming editions, clicking an acquisition link, and submitting Office Hours.

Microsoft Clarity

Microsoft Clarity helps us understand website experience through interaction analytics such as clicks, scrolling, mouse movement, navigation patterns, and reconstructed session behavior.

Clarity applies masking to information entered into text-input fields in its session-recording system.

Service providers

The Ivy Sanctum uses specialist providers to operate different parts of the website and business infrastructure.

Google Analytics

Website traffic, usage measurement, acquisition, and event analytics. Google Privacy Policy

Microsoft Clarity

Website interaction analytics, heatmaps, and session reconstruction. Microsoft Privacy Statement

Stripe

Payment processing, transaction administration, fraud prevention, and checkout. Stripe Privacy Policy

Formspree

Processing, routing, storage, and notification for website form submissions. Formspree Privacy Policy

Brevo

Branded email transmission and related email infrastructure. Brevo Privacy Policy

These providers operate under their own terms and privacy practices and may process information in locations associated with their infrastructure and service operations.

Cookies and similar technologies

Analytics and technology providers may use cookies, browser identifiers, and similar technologies to distinguish sessions, measure website use, maintain service functionality, and generate aggregated analytics.

Browser settings can be used to limit or remove cookies. Restricting cookies may reduce the continuity or completeness of certain analytics features.

Data retention

Information is retained for periods reasonably connected to the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to inquiries, delivering products or services, maintaining transaction and accounting records, resolving disputes, protecting the integrity of the business, and satisfying legal or regulatory requirements.

Different service providers maintain their own retention schedules for information processed through their systems.

Privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights relating to personal information, including access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection to certain processing.

Requests concerning information held directly by The Ivy Sanctum may be sent to hello@theivysanctum.com .

We may request reasonable information to confirm identity before completing a privacy request. Certain records may continue to be retained where required for accounting, taxation, legal compliance, fraud prevention, or the establishment or defense of legal claims.

Students and minors

The Ivy Sanctum serves an admissions audience that can include students under the age of eighteen.

The website is intended for users age thirteen and older. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under thirteen. A parent or guardian who believes information concerning a child under thirteen has been submitted may contact us to request review and deletion.

Security

The Ivy Sanctum uses reasonable technical and administrative measures appropriate to the nature of the information and relies on established infrastructure providers for website, communications, forms, analytics, and payments.

Internet transmission and electronic storage carry inherent security risks, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

International processing

The Ivy Sanctum and its service providers may process information in the United States and in other countries where those providers maintain operations or infrastructure. Privacy protections and legal requirements can differ across jurisdictions.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as The Ivy Sanctum adds products, publishing systems, analytics, communications infrastructure, or other services.

The date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

The Origin

Research, advisory, and a growing body of work.

The Ivy Sanctum is an independent admissions research and advisory practice. Its methodology draws on more than 30 years of combined experience across Harvard's schools, including firsthand experience reading files on a Harvard graduate admissions committee.

The methodology is developed through advisory work, frameworks, instruments, and publications in The Ivy Sanctum Library.
