The Sanctum
Origin

Research, advisory, and a growing body of work.

The Ivy Sanctum is an independent admissions research and strategy practice. Its methodology draws on more than 30 years of combined experience across Harvard’s schools, including firsthand experience reading files on a Harvard graduate admissions committee.

The work is applied through direct engagements each cycle while being developed into frameworks, instruments, and publications for applicants working independently.

Admissions is where the work begins.

Approach

The application is built as one coherent argument.

The Ivy Sanctum reads the full application as a connected evidence system: academic record, testing, honors, activities, recommendations, essays, supplements, context, and institutional positioning.

Each component carries a distinct function inside the larger file. The objective is not simply to strengthen individual pieces, but to make the evidence converge on a clear reader understanding.

Read → Architect → Position

The same method governs Profile Review and Writing Overhaul, with the depth and sequence determined by the application in front of us.
