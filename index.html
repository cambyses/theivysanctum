The Approach

The application is read as one argument.

Academics establish range. Activities create evidence. Essays give that evidence meaning. Recommendations provide witness. School-specific writing establishes placement.

Ivy Sanctum reads these signals together and builds strategy around the file as a whole: what carries authority, where the strongest proof sits, how the narrative should move, and which parts of the application should do the heaviest work.

Read

Establish how the file lands: signal, credibility, trajectory, pressure, and reader inference.

Architect

Define the governing argument and give each component a precise function inside the file.

Position

Align evidence, narrative, recommendations, and institutional fit into one coherent admissions case.

The Office

Applied strategy for the application in front of you.

Students usually arrive with a specific pressure point: an essay that has stalled, activities that feel scattered, recommenders who need direction, a school list that needs calibration, or a file that needs a stronger governing strategy.

The Office works directly on those problems through written diagnostics, essay and supplement architecture, activity framing, recommender strategy, and full-file planning.
